# Literature check: generation-level vehicle recognition with frozen encoders and prototypes

Date of access: 2026-11-06
Scope: confirm or refute the candidate gap registered in ADR-0004, "generation-level vehicle recognition with frozen visual encoders (DINOv3, SigLIP2, CLIP, OpenCLIP) and prototype-based retrieval, under limited support images and viewpoint mismatch, on a curated CompCars make-model-generation protocol".
Vocabulary follows `GLOSSARY.md` (generation, model-year class, support image, prototype, cross-view, Track A/B).

Method: every claim below was traced to the owning primary source (arXiv, MDPI, IEEE/OpenAlex metadata, CUHK dataset page, PDF full text). Where only an abstract or an index record was reachable, the row says so. Items marked UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed against the source on the access date and must not be cited as fact.

Verdict in one line: the gap holds as a **conjunction** (generation label on CompCars + frozen encoders + prototypes + support-size sweep + held-out viewpoint), but every individual ingredient except two has prior art. The two ingredients with no prior art found are (i) a curated generation label on CompCars and (ii) a held-out-viewpoint (cross-view) condition for fine-grained vehicle classification. Section 4 gives the wording consequences.

## 1. Primary sources checked first

### 1.1 Buzzelli & Segantin, Sensors 2021 — "Revisiting the CompCars Dataset for Hierarchical Car Classification"

Citation: Buzzelli, M.; Segantin, L. Revisiting the CompCars Dataset for Hierarchical Car Classification: New Annotations, Experiments, and Results. *Sensors* 2021, 21(2), 596. https://doi.org/10.3390/s21020596 (open access; full text read via Europe PMC XML of PMC7830427).

What was recorded:

- **Label levels.** Four levels on CompCars web-nature: *type* (12 classes; originally present for only 968 of 1,716 models, propagated by the authors to all models), *make* (163), *model* (1,716), and *make-model-year* "MMY" (4,455 classes, the finest level). Year is used exactly as CompCars records it; the authors do not merge years into generations.
- **"Generation".** The word does not appear in the paper body (searched in the full text; only "generalization" appears). Year is treated as a flat label. The paper comments that year is the hardest level because "some model years differ radically, while others differ only slightly and subtly".
- **Splits and their effect.** They note the official CompCars classification split covers only 30,955 images of 431 models and merges years. They build a *new split* over all 136,725 web-nature images: each image is embedded with ResNet-34 (512-d), the average distance to other images of its class is computed, and the 70 % nearest images go to train, the rest to test (95,710 train / 41,015 test). Effect: ResNet-50 (no augmentation, no cropping) reaches about **90 % MMY on a random split but 61 % on the new split**; they attribute the random-split number to near-duplicate images ("pictures that are extremely similar one to another") landing on both sides, and they suggest the very high accuracies reported by earlier work (Ma et al., Fukui et al.) reflect this bias.
- **Methods.** Fine-tuned ResNet-50 classifiers in three configurations: all-vs-all (AVA) over 4,455 MMY classes; two-step cascade (2SC) type then MMY; hierarchical multi-label (HML). Faster R-CNN (ResNet-101) detector for car-tight boxes. Rejection by confidence threshold.
- **Metrics / headline numbers (test, new split).** AVA: MMY 0.688, type 0.907. 2SC with BCE at type level: type 0.934, MMY **0.701** (best). HML: type 0.920, MMY 0.655. Oracle-type routing in 2SC: 0.738. Rejection threshold 59 % rejects 20.2 % of MMY predictions.
- **New annotations released.** Propagated type labels for all models; the new train/test split; automatically generated car-tight bounding boxes for all 136,725 images (not individually verified); per-image ROC data. Stated location: http://www.ivl.disco.unimib.it/activities/hierarchical-car-classification/ — **the host refused connections on 2026-11-06 (ECONNREFUSED)**; availability today is UNVERIFIED.
- **Viewpoints.** Not used as a label or as a split variable; handled only by augmentation (random rotation within 20 degrees, random translation).
- **Few-shot, frozen features, prototypes.** None. ResNet-34 features are used only to build the split.

Relevance to the gap: closest prior work on year-granular CompCars. It establishes the MMY baseline (0.70 fine-tuned, full supervision) and the duplicate-leakage warning that motivates the group-aware split in Track B. It does not define generations, does not freeze encoders, does not study support size, and does not hold out viewpoints.

### 1.2 NTOU-MMR and the "generation" line of work

Original dataset paper: Chen, L.-C.; Hsieh, J.-W.; Yan, Y.; Chen, D.-Y. Vehicle make and model recognition using sparse representation and symmetrical SURFs. *Pattern Recognition* 2015, 48(6), 1979–1998. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2014.12.018 (citation as given by Jamil et al. 2020; original not read; dataset host reported as mmplab.cs.ntou.edu.tw, UNVERIFIED).

Generation paper: Manzoor, M. A.; Morgan, Y.; Bais, A. Real-Time Vehicle Make and Model Recognition System. *Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction* 2019, 1(2), 611–629. https://doi.org/10.3390/make1020036. Abstract (OpenAlex record) confirms the system "recognizes make, model, and generation (manufacturing years)" and contrasts this with existing VMMR systems that identify only make and model. The MDPI full text returned HTTP 403 on the access date; the following details are taken from secondary citations of it (Jamil et al. 2020; Gayen et al. 2023 survey) and are marked accordingly: NTOU-MMR was re-labelled by the authors into **35 make-model-generation classes** (frontal surveillance images, Taiwanese market: Toyota, Ford, Mitsubishi, Honda, Suzuki, Nissan), with **2,725 training / 3,110 test images** (UNVERIFIED against the primary text), features HOG and GIST, classifiers Random Forest (94.53 %) and SVM (97.89 %).

Follow-ups on NTOU-MMR that do *not* use generation: Jamil, A. A.; Hussain, F.; Yousaf, M. H.; Butt, A. M.; Velastin, S. A. Vehicle Make and Model Recognition Using Bag of Expressions. *Sensors* 2020, 20(4), 1033. https://doi.org/10.3390/s20041033 — 29 make-model classes, frontal view (camera within ±20 degrees), ten random 80/20 splits, BRISK+HOG+BoE 98.43 %. They explicitly list NTOU-MMR defects: duplicated images saved under different labels, misplaced images, unclear train/test division. VGG16+GA+SVM work reports 98.2 % (as listed in the Gayen et al. survey).

Relevance to the gap: the only prior "generation" label in the VMMR literature that I could find is a hand-crafted-feature system on a small frontal-only surveillance dataset. Generation is defined there as "manufacturing years", i.e., the same notion as this project's curated interval, but without a provenance-backed mapping, without multiple viewpoints, without deep or frozen features, and without a support-size study.

A second, non-public generation label: Hayee, S.; Hussain, F.; Yousaf, M. H. A Novel FDLSR-Based Technique for View-Independent Vehicle Make and Model Recognition. *Sensors* 2023, 23(18), 7920. https://doi.org/10.3390/s23187920 — mentions a Pakistani on-road dataset of 129,000 images in 94 classes "labelled by make, model, and generation"; public release not stated. Their reported experiments are on Stanford Cars (94.62 % top-1) and BoxCars21k (98.88 %) with fine-tuned ResNet-152 features plus FDLSR+SVM/KNN. "View-independent" is asserted from dataset properties; no viewpoint is held out.

### 1.3 Yang, Luo, Loy, Tang — CompCars (CVPR 2015 / arXiv 1506.08959 v2)

Citation: Yang, L.; Luo, P.; Loy, C. C.; Tang, X. A Large-Scale Car Dataset for Fine-Grained Categorization and Verification. CVPR 2015; extended version arXiv:1506.08959v2 (24 Sep 2015). https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.08959 ; dataset page https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/datasets/comp_cars/ (full text read via ar5iv).

- **Composition.** Web-nature: 163 makes, 1,716 models, 136,727 whole-car images (136,726 on the dataset page) plus 27,618 part images; surveillance-nature: 44,481 (paper) / 50,000 (page) front-view images. Hierarchy stated as make / model / year of manufacture. Five viewpoints: front (F), rear (R), side (S), front-side (FS), rear-side (RS). Attributes: maximum speed, displacement, doors, seats, type.
- **Year.** "For each car model, the car images produced in different years are considered as a single category." The authors note that separating years would be harder and leave it unexplored.
- **Official classification split.** Section 4 (original): 431 models from Part-I, 30,955 whole-car images, half/half. Section 5 (updated, the split distributed in `arxiv_data.zip` / `train_test_split/classification`): same 431 models, 52,083 images, 70 % train / 30 % test. Later work (EP-CNN 2022) confirms the 70/30 version with 36,456 training images.
- **Results.** Per-viewpoint top-1 (Overfeat, original setup, trained per view): F 0.524, R 0.431, S 0.428, FS 0.563, RS 0.598, all-view 0.767. Updated all-view (70/30): AlexNet 0.819 / 0.940 top-5, Overfeat 0.879 / 0.969, GoogLeNet 0.912 / 0.981. Training always sees all viewpoints; no condition holds a viewpoint out of training.
- **Verification.** Part-III, easy/medium/hard pairs (easy = same viewpoint, medium = random viewpoints, hard = same make negatives); GoogLeNet + Joint Bayesian 0.907 / 0.852 / 0.788.

Relevance: establishes Track A exactly (431 classes, official Part-I split, years merged) and the viewpoint vocabulary. It contains no frozen-feature, prototype, few-shot, or held-out-view experiment.

## 2. Search sweep, 2015–2026 (notable earlier work included)

### 2.1 Foundation-model / frozen features for vehicle make-model(-year)

- **Delazeri, A. V.; Lima, G. E.; Nascimento, E. Jr.; Laroca, R.; Menotti, D. Evaluating 2D and 3D-Aware Vision Foundation Models for Vehicle Attribute Recognition. arXiv:2608.29929 (30 Aug 2026), accepted SIBGRAPI 2026.** 14 frozen encoders (DINOv1, iBOT, DINOv2, DINOv3, SAM 3, and nine 3D-aware models; no CLIP or SigLIP), linear and MLP probes on UFPR-VeSV (surveillance; type 14, make 26, model 136; no year). Few-shot = linear probes on 1/5/10/25 % of training data, type only. DINOv3 linear probe: make 93.5, model 95.0 macro-accuracy; DINOv2 65.9 / 66.5. They report DINOv3 type accuracy is sensitive to viewing angle (p<0.01) while Depth Anything v2 is not. No prototypes, no held-out view, no generation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29929
- **Lima et al. Toward Unified Fine-Grained Vehicle Classification and Automatic License Plate Recognition. arXiv:2604.05271 (7 Apr 2026), JBCS.** Introduces UFPR-VeSV; supervised CNN baselines; no year. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05271
- **Chang, W.-C.; Chen, Y.-A. Zero-Shot Vehicle Model Recognition via Text-Based Retrieval-Augmented Generation. arXiv:2510.18502 (21 Oct 2025), FRUCT 2025.** VLM describes the image, text retrieval + LM infers make and model; CLIP ViT-B/32 zero-shot baseline about 0.21 accuracy, pipeline about +18–20 %. Text-only support, no image prototypes, no year, no viewpoint handling. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18502
- **Kerdvibulvech, C. Multimodal AI model for zero-shot vehicle brand identification. *Multimedia Tools and Applications* 2025, 84(27), 33125–33144. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11042-024-20559-3.** Brand (make) only; abstract not retrievable (Springer login wall, OpenAlex abstract null). Content UNVERIFIED.
- **Muñoz, A.; Thomas, N.; Vapsi, A.; Borrajo, D. Veri-Car: Towards Open-world Vehicle Information Retrieval. arXiv:2411.06864 v4 (12 May 2025).** OpenCLIP ViT-B/16 (LAION-2B) image encoder **fine-tuned** with a hierarchical multi-similarity loss (text tower discarded, 128-d head). Stanford Cars classes (make+model+type+year); 160 seen / 36 unseen classes; recognition is k-NN retrieval against a gallery; unseen classes become recognisable after **8 labelled samples per class** are added (Prec@1 82.99 % unseen with 8 samples; seen 96.18 % Prec@1). Year is inside the class label but not analysed separately; no viewpoint handling. This is the nearest "open-world, few-support, embedding retrieval" vehicle paper, but the encoder is fine-tuned and the dataset is Stanford Cars. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06864
- **VehicleDINO (Hugging Face model card, wms2537/VehicleDINO).** DINOv2 ViT-B/14 with LoRA adapters on a CompCars subset (42 makes, 323 models): 98.4 % make, 87.7 % model. Not peer reviewed; adapters mean the encoder is not frozen. UNVERIFIED beyond the card. https://huggingface.co/wms2537/VehicleDINO
- **Miu (Pavel), A.-V.; Ionescu, B. Can we recognize Cars we've never seen? A Journey Through Zero-Shot Learning in Vehicle Recognition. 2nd AI4MM Workshop, 2025.** Survey of ZSL/VLMs for VMMR plus a proposed CLIP/SigLIP pipeline with prompt engineering, LoRA tuning and multi-view InfoNCE; merges CompCars, VeRi-776, VehicleID, VERI-Wild. No generation label, no prototype protocol, no results table in the PDF read. (Author's own prior work; does not pre-empt the present gap.) https://aimultimedialab.ro/ai4mm-workshop-2/6_Miu_Pavel_Adriana_AIM_Workshop_2025.pdf

Reference numbers for frozen encoders on cars (not vehicle-specific papers):

- **CLIP (Radford et al., 2021, arXiv:2103.00020).** Stanford Cars (196 make-model-year classes): zero-shot ViT-B/32 59.4, ViT-B/16 65.6, ViT-L/14 77.3, ViT-L/14@336 78.8; linear probe 81.8 / 86.7 / 90.9 / 91.5. Tables 10–11 read via ar5iv.
- **DINOv2 (Oquab et al., 2023, arXiv:2304.07193), Table 8 linear eval.** Cars: ViT-B/14 88.2, ViT-L/14 90.1, ViT-g/14 91.4; OpenCLIP ViT-G/14 96.1. CUB: 89.6 / 90.5 / 91.6 vs OpenCLIP 89.9. Language-supervised encoders lead on Cars; this is the a-priori reason SigLIP2/CLIP are included in E1.

### 2.2 Few-shot, nearest-centroid, prototypical vehicle recognition

- **Balci, B.; Artan, Y. Few-Shot Learning for Vehicle Make & Model Recognition: Weight Imprinting vs. Nearest Class Mean Classifiers. IEEE ITSC 2020. https://doi.org/10.1109/ITSC45102.2020.9294303.** Traffic-surveillance images; 100 base + 100 novel classes, 8,423 test images; NCM (class-mean prototype) 82 % overall vs weight imprinting 65 %. Backbone details and shot counts not in the abstract (IEEE page blocked); dataset private. Closest prior "prototype = class mean" VMMR paper. Fine-tuned backbone on base classes (inferred from the imprinting protocol; UNVERIFIED).
- **Wolf, S. Attention-Based Few-Shot Learning for Fine-Grained Vehicle Classification. KIT/Fraunhofer IOSB proceedings (c. 2020–2021), pp. 201–220.** Stanford Cars (130/17/49 class split) and CompCars SV (frontal, 140/71/70 classes); transfer, dynamic and prototypical networks with Conv4/ResNet-18, 1/5/10 shots, macro-F1 over novel classes with base classes present. ProtoNet ResNet-18 on Stanford Cars 18.4 / 34.9 / 38.0 F1; transfer 39.6 / 53.8 / 52.0; CompCars SV transfer ResNet-18 85.7 / 88.6 / 88.8. Attention helps on Stanford Cars (varied views) but not on CompCars SV (all frontal). Trained backbones, no frozen FM, no held-out view. https://publica-rest.fraunhofer.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/1fcb2e97-d717-42ca-8eaa-41edc371e2a4/content
- **Kezebou, L.; Oludare, V.; Panetta, K.; Agaian, S. Few-Shots Learning for Fine-Grained Vehicle Model Recognition. IEEE HST 2021. https://doi.org/10.1109/HST53381.2021.9619823.** RelationNet++, 1–20 shots per class; the Gayen et al. survey lists 83.5 % on CompCars with 959 classes (secondary; UNVERIFIED against the paper). Meta-trained, not frozen.
- **Yi, H. Few-Shot Class-Incremental Learning with Class Centers and Contrastive Learning for Incremental Vehicle Recognition. IJCNN 2024. https://doi.org/10.1109/IJCNN60899.2024.10650773.** "Incremental-frozen" framework: backbone trained in the base session with contrastive + center loss, then frozen; new classes inserted as class centers. Two fine-grained vehicle datasets (names not in abstract). Class-center prototypes over a frozen (but self-trained) backbone.
- **Picek, L.; Janouskova, K.; Cermak, V.; Matas, J. FungiTastic. arXiv:2408.13632, FGVC@CVPR 2025.** Reports that centroid prototypes on frozen DINOv2 embeddings beat nearest-neighbour in few-shot (search-snippet level; table not re-read). Not vehicles; cited only as evidence that "frozen FM + class-mean prototype" is an established baseline.
- **Goswami et al. Project and Mix: Task-Semantic Prototypes for Few-Shot Image Classification. arXiv:2603.24528 (2026)** and **Li et al. The Blending Ratio Is Not Where the Performance Is. arXiv:2608.23634 (2026).** Both treat the nearest-class-mean classifier on frozen CLIP/SigLIP image features as the standard training-free few-shot baseline across the 10–11 dataset suite that includes Stanford Cars (K = 1–16). Stanford Cars NCM rows not re-read here. They confirm that frozen-encoder prototype classification per se is not novel, including on a car dataset whose classes carry a year.

### 2.3 Viewpoint-robust, cross-view, multi-view fine-grained vehicle recognition

- **Chen, Q.; Liu, W.; Yu, X. A Viewpoint Aware Multi-Task Learning Framework for Fine-Grained Vehicle Recognition. *IEEE Access* 2020, 8, 171912–171923. https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3024658.** Viewpoint estimation as auxiliary task, BoxCars116k and CompCars; fine-tuned; all views in training.
- **Yu, Y.; Liu, H.; Fu, Y.; Jia, W.; Yu, J.; Yan, Z. Embedding Pose Information for Multiview Vehicle Model Recognition (EP-CNN). *IEEE TCSVT* 2022. https://doi.org/10.1109/TCSVT.2022.3151116.** CompCars web-nature, 431 models, 52,083 images, 70/30; viewpoint and pose features embedded into a MultiSE-ResNet-50; top-1 98.6 % (224 px) / 98.9 % (448 px); ResNet-50 baseline 94.0 %. Viewpoint is a conditioning signal, never held out. Fine-tuned.
- **Hayee et al. 2023 (above).** "View-independent" by dataset choice only.
- **Jamil et al. 2020 / Manzoor 2019.** Frontal only.
- **Vehicle re-identification analogues.** Chu et al., VANet (ICCV 2019) define viewpoint-related protocols (same-view vs different-view queries). **Ben Mabrouk, A. Y. et al. Generalization Limits in Vehicle Re-Identification. arXiv:2606.01981 (1 Jun 2026)** splits evaluation into same-view and different-view galleries and into seen vs unseen vehicle *types*, reporting drops of about 20 % on unseen types and at least 30 % under different-view evaluation (numbers from the search summary; UNVERIFIED against the PDF). Instance-level re-ID, not class-level generation recognition, and the held-out variable is type, not viewpoint.
- **Frozen-FM cross-view probes outside vehicles.** *Probing Fine-Grained Action Understanding and Cross-View Generalization of Foundation Models*, arXiv:2407.15605 (2024): linear probes trained on front-view video, tested on side/top views; accuracy falls from about 56 % to 15–25 % (search-level). *Not all Views are Created Equal* (Michalkiewicz et al., arXiv:2412.19920, Dec 2024): nine foundation models, feature instability under viewpoint change, accidental vs OOD viewpoints; no car dataset named. *VINE: View-Consistent Prototype Learning for Few-Shot Segmentation* (CVPR 2026): prototypes under viewpoint shift with a frozen backbone, but segmentation.

No paper was found in which a CompCars viewpoint label is removed from the training/support set and used only for queries, for either fine-tuned or frozen models.

### 2.4 Work defining generations or year ranges as classes on CompCars

None found. Yang et al. merge years; Buzzelli & Segantin use raw years (4,455 MMY classes). Searches for "generation", "facelift", "model year" with CompCars returned only these two and generic year-level work on Stanford Cars / VMMRdb / BoxCars (classes = make-model-year or make-model-submodel-year, never curated generation intervals). The EP-CNN failure-case analysis mentions that CompCars classes "Audi A4L, A6L, A7 are different vehicle models" (nameplate confusion), not generations.

### 2.5 Surveys

- **Gayen, S.; Maity, S.; Singh, P. K.; Geem, Z. W.; Sarkar, R. Two decades of vehicle make and model recognition – Survey, challenges and future directions. *J. King Saud Univ. – Comput. Inf. Sci.* 2024, 36(1), 101885 (published Dec 2023). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jksuci.2023.101885.** Lists viewpoint variation and the need for viewpoint-robust methods as open challenges; tabulates NTOU-MMR (29 and 35-class variants) and CompCars results; abstract read via OpenAlex, body not read (publisher 403).

## 4. Conclusions

### (a) How close the nearest prior work comes, item by item

1. **Generation label on CompCars.** No prior work defines generations on CompCars. Yang et al. merge years; Buzzelli & Segantin keep raw years (4,455 MMY classes) and never use the word "generation". The only published generation labels in VMMR are Manzoor et al. 2019 (35 classes, frontal surveillance, hand-crafted features; dataset itself has documented label defects) and a non-public Pakistani dataset in Hayee et al. 2023. **Distance: large.** The curated, provenance-backed, dual-reviewed generation mapping with facelift phases is new, provided the paper distinguishes it clearly from MMY and from Manzoor's "generation = manufacturing years".
2. **Frozen encoders.** Frozen DINOv2/DINOv3 features for make/model are benchmarked on UFPR-VeSV (Delazeri et al. 2026, linear probes, no CLIP/SigLIP, no year). CLIP/DINOv2/OpenCLIP numbers on Stanford Cars (make-model-year classes) are in the respective model papers. Nothing on CompCars with frozen encoders, nothing with SigLIP2 or DINOv3 on any year-granular car label. **Distance: moderate.** The encoder-comparison is incremental in method; its value lies in the label and protocol.
3. **Prototypes (class-mean, cosine).** Established baseline: NCM on frozen CLIP/SigLIP across suites that include Stanford Cars (2026 few-shot papers), centroid-vs-kNN on frozen DINOv2 (FungiTastic), NCM vs weight imprinting for VMMR (Balci & Artan 2020), class-center FSCIL for vehicles (Yi 2024), k-NN gallery retrieval with fine-tuned OpenCLIP for open-world make/model/year (Veri-Car). **Distance: small.** Prototype retrieval itself cannot be claimed as a contribution; view-balanced (M1) and view-conditioned (M2) prototypes evaluated under a held-out view are the only prototype-level novelty, and M2's idea has cousins in viewpoint-conditioned fine-tuned networks (EP-CNN, Chen et al. 2020) and view-conditioned re-ID (VANet).
4. **Few-shot / limited support.** Few-shot VMMR exists in several forms (Wolf; Balci & Artan; Kezebou; Yi; Veri-Car's 8-samples-per-unseen-class). None is at generation granularity, none uses a frozen foundation encoder on CompCars, and none sweeps support size against viewpoint composition. **Distance: moderate.**
5. **Cross-view (query viewpoint absent from references).** Not found for fine-grained vehicle classification, fine-tuned or frozen. Yang et al. report per-view accuracy with all views in training; EP-CNN and Chen et al. use the viewpoint as input. The nearest analogues are re-ID "different-view" galleries (VANet 2019; Ben Mabrouk et al. 2026) and cross-view probes of frozen FMs in action recognition (2407.15605). **Distance: large for VMMR, moderate in the wider frozen-FM literature**, where viewpoint sensitivity of frozen features is already documented (Michalkiewicz et al. 2024; Delazeri et al. 2026 report DINOv3 angle sensitivity on type).

Overall: the gap is real as a conjunction, and two of its five parts have no direct prior art. It is weaker than the ADR's phrasing on the prototype and few-shot parts.

### (b) Claims in ADR-0004 that are safe as written

- "how reliably frozen visual encoders distinguish generations of the same vehicle model when few labeled reference images are available and the query viewpoint differs from the references" — safe as the *question*; no prior paper answers it.
- "Protocol v1.1 had no condition in which the query viewpoint is absent from support. We add a cross-view condition ..." — safe; and the literature search found no such condition in VMMR classification, so it can be presented as new there.
- "The decisive experiment is M0 versus M1 prototypes on identical support ... wrong-generation/same-model error rate" — safe; the error decomposition (same model, wrong generation) is unreported elsewhere because no one has the label.
- "Track A remains the link to prior work" — safe, with the caveat in (c) about which Part-I split version is meant and the duplicate-leakage audit.
- "The gap is a candidate until the literature review ... confirms it against Buzzelli & Segantin (Sensors 2021) and the NTOU-MMR generation-recognition work" — this review confirms it, with the rewordings below.
- The working title's "Frozen Visual Encoders" and "Viewpoint-Aware Prototypes" are defensible; "Fine-Grained Vehicle Generation Recognition on CompCars" is accurate and currently unique.

### (c) Claims that need rewording, with suggested wording

1. **Do not present prototype-based retrieval over frozen encoders as a methodological contribution.** Suggested: "We use the standard nearest-class-mean (prototype) classifier over L2-normalised frozen embeddings [CLIP; DINOv2; Balci & Artan 2020] as the recognition rule, so that differences between encoders and between prototype constructions (global, view-balanced, view-conditioned) are not confounded by learned heads." Keep M5 (linear probe) as the supervised reference, as Delazeri et al. 2026 do.
2. **Qualify "few labeled reference images".** Few-shot VMMR is prior art (Wolf; Balci & Artan; Kezebou; Yi; Veri-Car's 8 samples/class). Suggested: "under support sizes from K = 1 to the full support set, which earlier few-shot VMMR studies report only for make-model classes and with task-trained backbones."
3. **Name the generation prior art precisely.** The ADR says "the NTOU-MMR generation-recognition work"; cite it as Manzoor, Morgan & Bais, MAKE 2019 (35 make-model-generation classes, frontal surveillance, HOG/GIST with SVM/RF) and add Hayee et al. 2023 (private 94-class make-model-generation set). Suggested sentence: "Generation-level labels have appeared only on a small frontal-view surveillance set with hand-crafted features [Manzoor 2019] and on a non-public dataset [Hayee 2023]; no multi-view benchmark with a documented generation mapping exists."
4. **Position against Buzzelli & Segantin explicitly.** They hold the year-granular CompCars baseline (MMY 0.70, fine-tuned ResNet-50, 4,455 classes, own split). Suggested: "Buzzelli & Segantin [2021] classify CompCars at make-model-year granularity with fine-tuned CNNs and show that random splits inflate accuracy through near-duplicate images (about 0.90 vs 0.61). We adopt their leakage concern through a group-aware duplicate split, replace raw model years with curated generations, and replace fine-tuning with frozen encoders and prototypes." Report an MMY-level secondary number or a short justification of why MMY is not a sensible target (facelift-only years), otherwise reviewers will ask for it.
5. **Cross-view: claim it for fine-grained vehicle classification, not in general.** Suggested: "To our knowledge no fine-grained vehicle classification protocol evaluates queries whose viewpoint is absent from the reference set; related protocols exist for vehicle re-identification (same-view vs different-view galleries [VANet 2019; Ben Mabrouk 2026]) and for cross-view probing of frozen features in action recognition [2407.15605]." Do not write "first cross-view evaluation of foundation models".
6. **Viewpoint-aware prototypes: acknowledge viewpoint-conditioned predecessors.** Suggested: "View-conditioned recognition has been studied with fine-tuned networks that take the viewpoint as input [Chen 2020; Yu 2022]; we instead condition only the prototype construction, leaving the encoder frozen."
7. **Track A wording.** Two official settings exist: the CVPR 2015 half/half split over 30,955 images and the arXiv v2 70/30 split over 52,083 images (36,456 train). **The split files present locally (`train_test_split/classification/train.txt` 16,016 lines, `test.txt` 14,939 lines, sum 30,955) match the CVPR half/half setting, not the 70/30 setting.** Track A must therefore be labelled "official CVPR 2015 half/half classification split (30,955 images, 431 models)", and comparisons with papers that use the 70/30 setting (EP-CNN 2022 and most later work) are *not* direct: the literature matrix (§21) must flag them `directly_comparable = no` with reason "different official split version". Report Track A numbers *with* the duplicate audit disclosed, because Buzzelli & Segantin argue the official split is biased. (Local-file counts verified 2026-11-06.)
8. **Encoder novelty.** "DINOv3 and SigLIP2 on CompCars" is unreported, but "frozen DINOv3 for vehicle make/model" is reported (Delazeri et al. 2026, surveillance data). Suggested: "DINOv3 has recently been shown to be the strongest frozen encoder for make/model on surveillance imagery [Delazeri 2026]; we extend the comparison to web-nature multi-view imagery, to language-supervised encoders (SigLIP2, CLIP, OpenCLIP), and to generation granularity."

Items that remain UNVERIFIED and should be checked before citation: Manzoor 2019 class and image counts (full text blocked); Balci & Artan backbone and K; Kezebou's 83.5 % figure; Kerdvibulvech 2025 content; Ben Mabrouk 2026 drop percentages; current availability of the Buzzelli & Segantin annotation page; VehicleDINO model card numbers.
